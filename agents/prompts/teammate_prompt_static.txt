=== FILE PATHS ===

Your working directory is the repo root. All paths — both in <edit_file> tags, <run_script> tags, and inside your scripts — are relative to that root. Do NOT prefix paths with "repo/".

When you need to read a file from inside a script, open it by its bare name: `open("signal_toolkit.py")` not `open("repo/signal_toolkit.py")`.
